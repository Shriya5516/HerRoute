Safety Tips for Solo Girls

Keep these tips in mind and travel worry-free.

Plan Ahead

Research your destination and plan your trip.

Share Itinerary

Share your travel plans with someone you trust.

Stay Connected

Keep your phone charged and internet active.

Avoid Isolated Areas

Avoid lonely or poorly lit areas, especially at night.

Trust Your Instincts

If something feels wrong, trust your instincts.

Keep Essentials

Carry safety tools like pepper spray, whistle, etc.

You are strong, independent and capable. Enjoy your journey! 💜
